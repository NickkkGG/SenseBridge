SenseBridge

One sense in,
another sense out.

SenseBridge watches and listens through a laptop's camera and microphone, then answers in the sense a student can use: a warning they can hear, captions they can read, and a voice for what they want to say.

Runs on
A laptop with a webcam
Speaks
Bahasa Indonesia, English
Built with
Python, CustomTkinter, OpenCV, MediaPipe, PyTorch, YOLOv8
Started as
AksesKita, by Farid

The four lines

Each page of the app is one line.

Tuna Netra

For blind and low-vision students

  1. The camera looks ahead
  2. YOLOv8n finds people, chairs, tables, vehicles and other everyday objects
  3. Box height estimates the distance; box position gives left, ahead or right
  4. You hearAwas! orang sangat dekat di kanan“Watch out, a person very close on your right,” then a beep

Tuna Rungu

For Deaf and hard-of-hearing students

  1. The microphone learns the room's noise for one second
  2. Speech is cut into sentences at each pause
  3. Google Speech turns every sentence into text
  4. You readHalo apa kabar, saya butuh bantuanLarge live captions with a history. Loud sounds flash an alert.

Tuna Wicara

For students with speech impairments

  1. Lips in front of the camera, or the keyboard
  2. MediaPipe finds the mouth and your lip model names the word
  3. Words gather in the message box, next to quick phrases
  4. Others hearHalo / Terima kasih / Tolong / Saya butuh bantuanRead aloud by the computer voice

Latih Model

Teaches the app your lips

  1. Record about 30 one-second clips of each word, plus _diam for a closed mouth
  2. Every clip is 24 frames of a 48 × 48 mouth crop
  3. A 3D CNN with a GRU learns them, with tempo, position and light shuffled
  4. You getmodels/lip_id.ptYour own lip model, used by Tuna Rungu and Tuna Wicara

Before you rely on it

  • An extra aid. Not a replacement for a cane or a guide. Distance is an estimate, and stairs, holes and glass are not detected.
  • Lip reading is personal. It knows only the words you recorded and works best for the person who recorded them.
  • Captions need the internet. Speech goes to Google Speech to become text.
  • Faces stay on the laptop. Recordings and trained models are never uploaded with the code.

Run it

You need a laptop with a webcam and Python 3.11 or 3.12. Inside the project folder:

python3.12 -m venv .venv
.venv/bin/python -m pip install -r requirements.txt
.venv/bin/python main.py

On Windows, use .venv\Scripts\python instead. The code lives in a private GitHub repository for now.
